UFOs

Project Blue Book

Investigation · U.S. Air Force records
Bottom line: Project Blue Book was a real U.S. Air Force investigation of reported UFO sightings. The existence of unidentified reports does not by itself establish an extraterrestrial explanation.

What was Project Blue Book?

Blue Book was the Air Force's best-known formal UFO investigation program. It collected and evaluated reports from across the United States and elsewhere.

What is documented?

According to the National Archives, Blue Book investigated 12,618 reported sightings, of which 701 remained unidentified.

What does “unidentified” mean?

An unidentified case means investigators did not determine a conventional explanation from the available information. It does not mean the case was proven to involve extraterrestrial technology.

Why does it matter?

The surviving records provide an unusually large historical dataset for examining how government investigators handled unusual aerial reports.

Our assessment

Claim status: Documented investigation; extraterrestrial interpretation unproven.
