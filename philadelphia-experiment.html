CONSPIRACY

The Philadelphia Experiment

Investigation · Historical claim
Bottom line: The Philadelphia Experiment is a widely circulated story claiming that a U.S. Navy ship was made invisible or teleported in 1943. No reliable documentary evidence establishes that the experiment occurred as described.

What is claimed?

The legend says the USS Eldridge was subjected to an experimental technology intended to make it invisible to enemy detection. Later versions of the story add teleportation, severe effects on sailors, and other extraordinary details.

What is documented?

The USS Eldridge existed. The Naval History and Heritage Command says it was commissioned on 27 August 1943 and that its wartime records place it in New York and Long Island Sound, Bermuda, and later convoy service—not Philadelphia during the period described by the legend. The Navy also confirms that degaussing was real wartime technology used to reduce a ship's magnetic signature against magnetic mines. [1][2]

What evidence exists?

The Office of Naval Research states that the modern Philadelphia Experiment story traces to correspondence sent to author Morris K. Jessup in the 1950s by a person using the name Carlos Miquel Allende. The ONR information sheet says the identity of Allende was not established in the records it reviewed and that the alleged newspaper report described in the correspondence could not be identified. [2]

What remains disputed?

There are real historical subjects that can be confused with parts of the legend, including naval degaussing and other wartime electrical research. However, the Naval History and Heritage Command reports that repeated searches of Navy archival material have not located official documents confirming an invisibility or teleportation experiment. [1]

Our assessment

Claim status: Unsupported. The underlying historical setting is real, but the extraordinary elements of the Philadelphia Experiment are not established by reliable evidence. The strongest available official sources actually contradict key parts of the story, particularly the claimed location and movement of USS Eldridge in 1943. [1][3]
