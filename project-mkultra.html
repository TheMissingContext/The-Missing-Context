Project MKUltra

Investigation · CIA program
Bottom line: MKUltra was a real CIA program involving research into behavioral modification and related subjects. The documented record is extensive, although many records were destroyed in 1973.

What was MKUltra?

MKUltra was a CIA program that began in the early 1950s and involved research into methods that could affect human behavior, including work involving drugs and other techniques.

What is documented?

Declassified and surviving records establish that the program existed and that research was conducted through numerous subprojects and outside institutions.

Why is it associated with conspiracy theories?

Because the program involved secret funding arrangements, human experimentation concerns, and destroyed records, it became a major subject of later allegations about government experimentation.

What remains unknown?

The destruction of records means the surviving archive is incomplete. That limitation should not be used to automatically treat every later allegation as established fact.

Our assessment

Claim status: Documented. The existence and broad nature of MKUltra are historical facts. Individual claims about particular experiments should be evaluated separately.
